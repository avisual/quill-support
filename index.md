# Quill

**Quill asks for one permission: your microphone.**

Quill is a macOS app that turns your voice into text on your Mac. Hold your shortcut key, speak, let go — your words appear where your cursor is, in TextEdit, Chrome, Terminal and other apps that accept typed text. Speech is transcribed on-device — the Parakeet speech model by default, or Apple's built-in speech recognition — and your voice is never sent to the cloud.

## Key Features

- **One permission** — Quill asks for your microphone and nothing else. No Accessibility, no keyboard monitoring, no clipboard access, no screen access.
- **Completely local processing** — Your audio is transcribed on-device. No servers, no uploads, no cloud.
- **Runs on Apple Silicon** — transcription happens on your Mac.
- **Hold your shortcut key, let go** — set the shortcut once; Quill shows you exactly where to click.
- **English dictation** — tested and supported for English.

## How It Works

1. Set up a shortcut once — Quill shows you exactly where to click.
2. **Hold your shortcut key and speak.**
3. **Let go** — Quill transcribes on your Mac and types your words where your cursor is.

In Terminal with Secure Keyboard Entry and in password fields, press the key again to finish.

**Your voice and your words never leave your Mac. Quill only goes online to download its speech model.**

## Requirements

- macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later
- Apple Silicon (M1 or later) recommended
