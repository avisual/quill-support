# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: March 22, 2026**

Murmur is built on a simple principle: your voice stays on your device. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your data.

## What Data We Collect

**None.** Murmur does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Specifically:

- **Audio data** is processed entirely on your device using WhisperKit. No audio is recorded, uploaded, or sent to any server.
- **Transcriptions** remain on your device. Murmur does not store transcription history or send transcribed text anywhere.
- **Usage data** is not collected. There are no analytics, telemetry, or tracking of any kind.
- **Account information** does not exist. Murmur does not require or support user accounts.

## How We Use Your Data

We don't. All speech-to-text processing happens locally on your Mac using on-device machine learning models. Your audio never leaves your device.

## Network Activity

The only network activity Murmur performs is downloading machine learning models from [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/) when you first set up the app or choose a new model. This is a one-time download. Once models are stored locally, Murmur does not require an internet connection to function.

No audio, transcription text, or personal information is included in this download.

## Third-Party Services

Murmur does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

The app uses [WhisperKit](https://github.com/argmaxinc/WhisperKit), an open-source framework that runs entirely on-device. ML models are hosted by Hugging Face and downloaded directly to your Mac.

## Data Retention

Murmur does not retain any user data. Audio is processed in real time and is not saved. Transcriptions are delivered to your active application and are not stored by Murmur.

## Children's Privacy

Murmur does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Since Murmur collects no data, meaningful changes to this policy are unlikely.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/avisual/murmur-support/issues).
